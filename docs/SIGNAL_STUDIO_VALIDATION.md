# Signal Studio Visual Validation

The public marketplace has been reviewed at desktop and 390 px phone widths after the original Signal Studio redesign.

The new public identity uses mineral white, electric cobalt, vermilion, chartreuse, and pale sky surfaces. It does not reproduce the EventPulse composition, content, assets, or component structure. Project Rely retains its own brief-first marketplace, client project-posting flow, specialist evidence, workboard, and protected role workflows.

At desktop width, the redesign provides a strong open-product hierarchy: a framed opportunity hero, color-indexed discipline tiles, structured ledger cards, cobalt/vermilion decision states, and material shifts between discovery, method, workboard, and closing areas. At mobile width, the hero, actions, image card, category index, project ledger, specialist evidence, workboard, conversation, and closing call to action all stack without horizontal overflow.

Motion remains purposeful: existing Framer Motion entrances are retained, while the Signal Studio layer adds low-cost card response, a route-like rotating hero detail, and staggered hero entry behavior gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.
