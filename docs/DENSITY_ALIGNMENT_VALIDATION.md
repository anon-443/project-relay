# Density and Alignment Validation

The homepage was reviewed at **1919 × 1000** and **390 × 844** after the project-card and workboard density pass. At desktop size, each project title and description uses the available row width, while the budget, format, deadline, and brief action form a compact left-aligned facts strip. The prior detached right-side whitespace is removed.

The workboard uses larger icons and labels with shorter inactive panels. Project status is a full-width, left-led strip, and the conversation panel now uses a compact left rail, enlarged message text, and a wider message thread. The redundant helper sentence above the conversation was removed. On mobile, facts stack cleanly, the workboard becomes a denser single column, and the conversation remains readable without overflow.
