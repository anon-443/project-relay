# Specialist Card Density Validation

The featured-talent cards were reviewed at **1919 × 1000** and **390 × 844** after the density pass. This makes the text block a deliberate counterweight to the 480–560 px landscape image. A fresh **390 × 844** review confirmed that the mobile composition remains clear, with the landscape image, heading, and specialist-card sequence retaining their readable single-column flow.
